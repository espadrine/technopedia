Causal Inference: Do-Operator & Back-Door Path

Structural Causal Model

Observational mode: We observe units that happen to have X=1. Since U causes both X and Y, the observed group is biased (higher U → higher Y). The association is confounded.

Distribution of Y

P(Y | X=1) — associational (biased)
P(Y | do(X=1)) — interventional (true effect)
