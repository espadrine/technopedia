# Probability and statistics

Probability and statistics studies uncertainty, random behavior, and inference from data. It is relevant whenever a problem involves randomness, distributions, stochastic processes, statistical inference, or the quantification of uncertainty. It underlies data science, machine learning, physics, finance, and decision-making.

## Brief history

- 17th century: Pascal and Fermat develop the theory of games of chance.
- 18th–19th century: Bayes, Laplace, and Gauss formalize probability and statistical inference.
- Early 20th century: Kolmogorov axiomatizes probability on measure-theoretic foundations.
- Mid-20th century onward: stochastic processes, information theory, and computational statistics expand the field.
