1 · Theory of computation

Least-squares line fit is decidable and in P: the closed form is O(n); gradient descent is O(n·T) for T steps.

2 · Search & data structures

Points live in an array; each gradient step reads all n points → O(n). n = 0.

3 · Graphics

The canvas is the framebuffer: n points and the fitted line are rasterized into it.

4 · Optimization

Minimize L(m,b) = Σ(yᵢ − (m·xᵢ+b))² by gradient descent.
