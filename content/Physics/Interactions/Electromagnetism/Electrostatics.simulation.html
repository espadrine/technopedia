Electrostatics: drag the charges, watch the field and potential

Drag a charge to move it. Click “Add +/−” then click the canvas to place a new charge; double-click (or right-click) a charge to remove it. Field mode shows E as arrows (colour and length = strength); Potential mode shows V as a colour map (red +, blue −) with dotted equipotential contours. Note that the field is always perpendicular to the equipotentials.
